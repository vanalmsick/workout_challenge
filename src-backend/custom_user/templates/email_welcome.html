Getting started
Three steps
1 Join a friend's competition or create your own.
2 Link Strava for automatic workout imports – or log workouts by hand.
3 Pick a team before the competition starts.
 
How points work
Every competition sets its own activity goals. You earn 1 point for every 1% of a goal you complete.
A 100 minute goal and a 50 minute workout means 50 points.
Some goals carry minimum or maximum limits. Anything outside them earns no points and is marked with an asterisk (*). Hover a goal in the app to see its limits.
